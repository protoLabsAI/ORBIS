# Set and manage reminders

ORBIS can hold time-based reminders — one-off or recurring — and speak them when
they fire. You manage them entirely by voice, with the **reminders bell** as the
visual surface.

## Set one

Just ask:

> *"Remind me to stretch in 20 minutes."*
> *"Every weekday at 9, remind me to check the board."*

The orb confirms it. When the time comes, it **speaks the reminder** at the next
natural pause.

## See what's scheduled

- Ask: *"What reminders do I have?"*
- Or click the **reminders bell** (top-right, below the gear). A dot — tinted to
  the orb's colour — appears when any are set; the popover lists them.

## Cancel one (or all)

- By voice: *"Cancel the stretch reminder."*
- In the bell: hit the trash icon on a reminder, or **Clear all**.

Recurring reminders keep firing until you cancel them — handy for routines, but
remember to clear a test reminder you no longer want.
