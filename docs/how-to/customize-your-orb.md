# Customize your orb

Make the orb look the way you want — from a one-click palette to hand-tuned
parameters. Everything here is in **Settings → Orb**. For what each control
does, see the [Orb reference](/reference/orb).

## Quick: pick a variant and palette

1. Open **Settings → Orb**.
2. **Variant** — choose a rendering style (Fractal, Crystal, Galaxy, …). The
   orb changes immediately.
3. **Palette** — choose a colour scheme for that variant. Want it on-brand? Pick
   **ProtoLabs** (the lavender→indigo brand palette, on every variant).

That's enough for most people. Read on to fine-tune.

## Fine-tune the look

Below the palette, the variant's parameters are grouped into collapsible
sections — **Color**, **Energy**, **Motion**, and variant-specific controls.
Drag a slider and the orb updates live:

- **Color** — primary and secondary energy colours.
- **Energy** — density, glow, halo thickness/scale.
- **Motion** — internal speed, rotation, animation speed.

::: tip Feeling stuck? Randomize
Hit **Randomize** to roll a fresh set of parameters for inspiration, then tweak
from there. It won't touch your resolution setting.
:::

## Tune a specific state

The orb can look different while it's *listening*, *thinking*, or *speaking*.
To adjust one of those without changing the base look, switch the **authoring
context** from *Base* to the state you want, then edit — you're now editing that
state's overrides.

## Save it as a preset

Happy with it? Save it as a named **preset** (scoped to the current variant) so
you can come back to it. Switch variants and your presets for each are kept
separately.

## If the orb feels heavy

Some variants are GPU-intensive. Lower **Resolution (DPR)** in the orb's perf
controls to trade a little crispness for smoother motion and lower power use.
